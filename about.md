Alien Arboreal is based in Ottawa, Ontario. We work primarily with crested geckos, maintaining permanent animal IDs, lineage records, weights, breeding histories, and husbandry observations.

## Our approach

We are interested in the animals in front of us—not merely the label attached to them. That means tracking development, temperament, feeding, clutch intervals, incubation variables, and anything else that may help us make better decisions.

The goal is simple: produce healthy animals, represent them honestly, and leave better records than we inherited.

## Placement

We want each gecko to land in a prepared, lasting home. We are happy to discuss enclosure setup, feeding, temperament, and whether a particular animal is genuinely a good fit.
